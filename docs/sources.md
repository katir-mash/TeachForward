# Requirements review

Verified against official Texas Education Agency pages on **November 14, 2026**. These are manually reviewed snapshots; the application does not claim live monitoring. URLs and verification dates are included in `shared/roadmap.js` and shown in the product.

The profile intentionally does not establish certificate validity, degree equivalency, a grade band, exam approval, admission, costs, or a completion date. Those unknowns remain visible. The app does not choose exam codes from a broad subject interest or promise certification, employment, an internship, a test exemption, or admission.

The foreign-credential webpage and its linked checklist can differ as guidance changes. Prefer the current primary page and confirm document/evaluation details with TEA; no evaluator or evaluation-provider membership is hardcoded. An international degree without a teaching credential receives a separate clarification route.

Skills exercises and weekly planning are TeachForward suggestions, clearly distinguished from certification requirements. Checking tasks records the user’s own progress; it does not verify official eligibility or issue a certificate.
